HOA Architectural Review Guidelines V12 • 2026

ARC Application & Submittal Guide

A community-built completion aid designed to help Tracy Hills Phase 2 residents prepare complete, accurate, and guideline-aligned ARC submittals to avoid common review delays.

Download Master Submittal Template (.docx) View Architectural Guidelines V12 (Reference Copy) (PDF)
Do Not Begin Work Before Written Approval: Official governing documents mandate that written ARC approval must be obtained prior to commencing any exterior addition, construction, landscaping, demolition, or modification. Starting work prior to approval is a violation of Association CC&Rs and may result in fines or mandatory restoration. If work has already started or been completed without required ARC approval, contact Seabreeze Management directly regarding the appropriate compliance process.
ARC Approval vs City of Tracy Permits: ARC approval addresses compliance solely with Association governing documents. It does not replace building permits, zoning compliance, or inspections required by the City of Tracy. Conversely, obtaining a City permit does not waive the requirement for written ARC approval.
Discretionary Review Notice: This guide is a volunteer community reference designed to help residents prepare complete, guideline-aligned packages. It does not guarantee ARC approval. Each lot is reviewed on its specific merits, topography, and conditions. The Association's currently adopted governing documents and official instructions from Seabreeze Management Company always control.

Determine Your Review Classification

Identify which of the four submittal classifications applies to your scope of work before preparing your application package:

Tier 1 $0 Fee

1. Exempt Improvements

Only for improvements specifically identified as exempt under guideline conditions (e.g., minor seasonal planting of annual flowers, original builder touch-up paint, portable patio furniture, potted plants).

Tier 2 Notification Required

2. Pre-Approved Improvements

No prior written ARC approval is generally required, but the required application/notification must still be submitted to Management before work begins, and the improvement must conform strictly to the applicable pre-approved criteria (e.g., matching replacement of side-yard gates or garage doors).

Tier 3 $175 Review Fee

3. Minor Modifications

ARC review for specific minor modifications (e.g., repainting home exterior with a different approved color, low-profile storage sheds sitting entirely below the fence line, or post-mounted pathway lighting).

Tier 4 $200 Fee + $250 Deposit

4. Full / Major Projects

All major exterior projects, including all new rear/side yard landscaping, concrete patios, retaining walls, pergolas/patio covers, swimming pools, outdoor kitchens, gas fire pits, and water features.

Timelines & Review Deadlines

  • Required [V12 §III.A] Initial Landscape Submittal Deadline: Initial landscape improvement plans must be submitted to the ARC within 90 days of close of escrow.
  • Required [V12 §III.A] Initial Landscape Completion Deadline: All rear and side yard landscaping must be completed within 180 days of close of escrow.
  • Required [V12 §III.D] ARC Review Window: The ARC has up to 45 calendar days from the receipt of a complete application package to approve or deny the application. Incomplete submittals may be returned or denied and must be completed before the review can proceed. The governing documents contain additional procedures regarding failure to act within the applicable period; contact Management if the review period has expired.
  • Required [V12 §III.F] Construction Window: For approved improvements other than initial landscape improvements, work must substantially commence within 12 months of ARC approval and be completed within 6 months of commencement.

Submittal Fees & Security Deposit

Review Fee: $200 Full / $175 Minor

Non-refundable review fee payable to Tracy Hills II Community Association ($200 for full landscaping/hardscape, $175 for minor projects).

Security Deposit: $250 (Refundable, subject to applicable deductions)

Separate check payable to Tracy Hills II Community Association. The $250 security deposit is generally returned after the Notice of Completion is approved, subject to any permitted deductions for property damage, fines, legal fees, or other authorized amounts under the governing documents.

Application Preparation Steps

1

Update Red Placeholders

Open the Word document and replace all red placeholders (e.g., [LIKE THIS]) with your specific details: full name, street address, lot number, tract number, phone, email, and neighbor details.

2

Select Your Improvements

Review Section 2 and Form C. Mark [X] Yes for items you are installing and [X] No for items you are not installing. Customize text to accurately reflect project-specific conditions.

3

Attach Drawings, Photos & Product Cuts

  • • Exhibit E (Drawings): Insert your dimensioned 2D site layout (Sheet L-1) showing lot lines, setbacks, and drainage arrows. Include Name, Address, Lot #, and North Arrow.
  • • Exhibit F (Site Photos): Add clear photos of current lot (front and rear elevations, rear yard, each side yard, and surrounding conditions).
  • • Exhibit G (Product Cuts): Paste catalog cuts, spec sheets, or material swatches into the boxes for selected amenities, and delete unused boxes.
4

Collect Neighbor Signatures & Sign Forms

Neighbor signatures on Form D verify notification only, not permission or approval. If an adjacent or rear lot is unoccupied, under construction, or builder-owned, simply write 'Builder / Vacant Lot' on the signature line.

Sign and date Form B (Checklist), Form C (Request), Form D (Neighbor Notification), and Form G (Water Quality BMP Agreement).

Construction & Notice of Completion (Form E)

Build Exactly as Approved: For approved improvements other than initial landscape improvements, work must substantially commence within 12 months of ARC approval and be completed within 6 months of commencement. Essential Standards & Common Delay Factors

Technical specifications organized by requirement level and section citations to help prevent application delays:

Patio Covers, Pergolas & Shade Structures

Required [V12 §IV.B.6 & FPP Appendix B / CBC Ch 7A] Requires Full ARC Review. Phase 2 properties are subject to California Building Code Chapter 7A wildfire ignition-resistance standards. Minimum setbacks: 3'-0" from side property lines; 5'-0" from rear property line (7'-0" from rear property line if adjacent to a street).
Recommended Heavy-gauge extruded aluminum is a recommended non-combustible approach for typical Phase 2 applications to satisfy fire-hardening standards and prevent review delays. Include manufacturer spec cuts and dimensioned framing details.
Verify If proposing alternative or fire-retardant treated wood assemblies, verify compliance with FPP Appendix B and submit full engineering/fire rating specifications.

Retaining Walls & Planter Curbs

Required [V12 §IV.B.5.i] Maximum permitted height of retaining walls and terraced curbs is 24 inches (2 feet) from finished grade. Retaining walls are not permitted adjacent to the existing Residence.
Required [V12 §IV.B.3] Where soil is mounded against an existing wall or a raised planter occurs, provide required clearance (18" clear of perimeter walls) and adequate waterproofing/drainage. Thoroseal or equivalent waterproofing treatment is required where specified.
Verify Walls exceeding 24 inches or structural site retaining conditions require civil engineering calculations, City grading permits, and major ARC review.

Tree Setbacks & Root Barriers

Required [V12 §IV.D.6] Tree Setback: Proposed trees should be kept at least 5'-0" from property lines and boundary fences.
Required [V12 §IV.D.6] Root Barrier: Trees planted within 5 feet of perimeter walls and/or adjoining residences must incorporate an approved root-barrier device.
Recommended Show tree species, container size, installed location, and mature canopy diameter on site plans.

Phase 2 Fire Protection Plan: Prohibited Plants & Trees (Appendix A)

Required [FPP Appendix A] Proposed vegetation must comply with the complete prohibited-plant schedule and applicable fuel-modification requirements. Prohibited species include, among others, Cypress, Palms, Pines, Junipers, Bamboo, and Eucalyptus. Do not rely on summary examples alone; verify every proposed species against the complete official Appendix A schedule below.
Recommended Select from proven non-pyrophytic trees and ornamentals (Crape Myrtle, Japanese Maple, Western Redbud, Fruit Trees, Star Jasmine).
Verify Vegetation selection and fuel modification must comply with the complete Phase 2 FPP Appendix A schedule.

Drainage & Grading

Required [V12 §IV.B.3 / Form G] Maintain established lot drainage patterns. Hardscape surfaces require minimum 1.0% positive drainage; landscape areas require minimum 2.0% positive drainage directed away from structures. Do not direct runoff or sheet flow onto adjacent lots. New drainage inlets and sub-drains must connect to the existing drainage system provided by the Declarant.
Recommended Indicate surface slope arrows, elevations where applicable, and catch basin locations on Sheet L-1.

Existing Site Photos (Form B)

Required [Form B] Existing Site Photos: Include clear color photographs of the front elevation, rear yard, both side yards, and any surrounding conditions relevant to the proposed improvement. Add additional photographs where needed to fully document the project area.

Product Specifications (Exhibit G)

Required [Form B / ARC Review Mandate] Attach visual manufacturer specification sheets, catalog cuts, or material sample swatches for all proposed improvements (pergolas, stone veneer, flatwork, sheds, turf, etc.) in Exhibit G to prevent an incomplete package notice.
