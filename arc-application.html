HOA Architectural Review Guidelines V12 • 2026

ARC Application & Submittal Guide

A community-built completion aid designed to help Tracy Hills Phase 2 residents prepare complete, accurate, and guideline-aligned ARC submittals to avoid common review delays.

Download Master Submittal Template (.docx) View Official Guidelines V12 (PDF)
⚠️
Do Not Begin Work Before Written Approval: The governing documents mandate that written ARC approval must be obtained prior to commencing any exterior addition, construction, landscaping, demolition, or modification. Starting work prior to approval is a violation of Association CC&Rs and may result in fines or mandatory restoration. Work already in progress or completed may be subject to a separate compliance process and must be discussed directly with Seabreeze Management.
⚠️
ARC Approval ≠ City of Tracy Permits: ARC approval addresses compliance solely with Association governing documents. It does not replace building permits, zoning compliance, or inspections required by the City of Tracy. Conversely, obtaining a City permit does not waive the requirement for written ARC approval.
ℹ️
Discretionary Review Notice: This guide is a volunteer community reference designed to help residents prepare complete, guideline-aligned packages. It does not guarantee ARC approval. Each lot is reviewed on its specific merits, topography, and conditions. The Association's currently adopted governing documents and official instructions from Seabreeze Management Company always control.

Determine Your Review Classification

Identify which of the four submittal classifications applies to your scope of work before preparing your application package:

🟢

1. Exempt (No Review Required)

$0 Fee

Only for improvements specifically identified as exempt under guideline conditions (e.g., minor seasonal planting of annual flowers, original builder touch-up paint, portable patio furniture, potted plants).

🟢

2. Pre-Approved (Application Required)

Filing Required

No prior discretionary committee meeting is generally required, but an official application/notification must still be submitted to Management before work starts, and the installation must conform strictly to pre-approved criteria (e.g., identical replacement of side-yard gates to match the original builder gate).

🟡

3. Minor Review

$175 Fee

ARC review for specific minor modifications (e.g., repainting home exterior with a different approved color, low-profile storage sheds sitting entirely below fence line, or post-mounted pathway lighting).

🔴

4. Full Review

$200 Fee + $250 Deposit

All major improvements, including all new rear/side yard landscaping, concrete patios, retaining walls, pergolas/patio covers, swimming pools, outdoor kitchens, gas fire pits, and water features.

Timelines & Review Deadlines

  • 🔴 Required [V12 §III.A] Initial Landscape Submittal Deadline: Initial landscape improvement plans must be submitted to the ARC within 90 days of close of escrow.
  • 🔴 Required [V12 §III.A] Initial Landscape Completion Deadline: All rear and side yard landscaping must be completed within 180 days of close of escrow.
  • 🔴 Required [V12 §III.D] ARC Review Window: The ARC has 45 calendar days from the receipt of a complete application package to approve or deny the application. Incomplete submittals may be returned or denied and must be resubmitted completely before review proceeds.
  • 🔴 Required [V12 §III.F] Construction Window: Approved work must commence within 12 months and be completed within 6 months of commencement.

Submittal Fees & Security Deposit

Review Fee: $200 Full / $175 Minor

Non-refundable review fee payable to Tracy Hills II Community Association ($200 for full landscaping/hardscape, $175 for minor projects).

Security Deposit: $250 (100% Refundable)

Separate check payable to Tracy Hills II Community Association. 100% refundable upon final inspection via Form E verifying no damage to common areas.

Step-by-Step Instructions: Preparing Your Application

1

Update Bracketed Red Text

Open the Word document and replace all red placeholders (e.g., [LIKE THIS]) with your specific details: full name, street address, lot number, tract number, phone, email, and neighbor names/addresses.

2

Select Your Improvements (Modular Menu)

Review Section 2 and Form C. Mark [X] Yes for items you are installing and [X] No for items you are not installing. Customize text to accurately reflect project-specific conditions.

3

Drop in Drawings, Lot Photos & Product Cuts

  • • Exhibit E (Drawings): Insert your dimensioned 2D site layout (Sheet L-1) showing lot lines, setbacks, and drainage arrows. Include Name, Address, Lot #, and North Arrow.
  • • Exhibit F (Existing Site Photos): Add clear photos of current lot (front and rear elevations, rear yard, each side yard, and surrounding conditions).
  • • Exhibit G (Product Cuts & Swatches): Paste catalog cuts, spec sheets, or material swatches into the boxes for selected amenities, and delete unused boxes.
4

Collect Neighbor Signatures & Execute Forms

Neighbor signatures on Form D verify notification only, not permission or approval. If an adjacent or rear lot is unoccupied, under construction, or builder-owned, simply write 'Builder / Vacant Lot' on the signature line.

ℹ️ Sign and date Form B (Checklist), Form C (Request), Form D (Neighbor Notification), and Form G (Water Quality BMP Agreement).

Final Pre-Flight Check: Approval-Ready Checklist

Verify these items before submitting your package to Management to prevent delays or returns:

5

Submit to Management

Convert your completed package into a single consolidated PDF file. Submit according to current Seabreeze Management Company instructions (csteam@seabreezemgmt.com, Attn: ARC Team). Confirm current mailing address and deposit check procedures prior to submission.

💳 Two Separate Checks Required (Payable to 'Tracy Hills II Community Association'):
1. $200.00 Review Fee (or $175.00 for minor reviews).
2. $250.00 Security Deposit (100% refundable upon final inspection via Form E verifying no damage to common areas).
📬 Mailing Address: Tracy Hills II Community Association c/o Seabreeze Management Company, 4305 Hacienda Suite 350, Pleasanton CA 94588.
6

Construction & Post-Approval Lifecycle

Build Exactly as Approved: Work must commence within 12 months and be completed within 6 months. Any deviation or alteration from approved plans requires prior written approval from the ARC.
Notice of Completion (Form E): Upon finishing construction, complete and submit Form E (Notice of Completion) along with photographs of the completed improvements to Seabreeze Management.
Deposit Refund Process: Following review/final inspection by the ARC confirming no damage to Association property or unapproved deviations occurred, the $250 security deposit will be refunded to the homeowner.

Essential Standards & Common Delay Factors

Technical specifications organized by requirement level and section citations to help prevent application delays:

☂️ Patio Covers, Pergolas & Shade Structures

🔴 Required [V12 §IV.B.6 & FPP App B / CBC Ch 7A] Requires Full ARC Review. Phase 2 properties are also subject to the Phase 2 Fire Protection Plan and California Building Code Chapter 7A wildfire ignition-resistance standards. Minimum setbacks: 3'-0" from side property lines, 5'-0" from rear property line (7'-0" from rear property line if adjacent to a street).
🟡 Recommended Non-combustible heavy-gauge extruded aluminum is a commonly suitable, fire-safe approach for Phase 2 applications and avoids combustible material inquiries. Clearly specify manufacturer cut sheets, frame colors, dimensions, and non-combustible certifications.
🔵 Verify If proposing fire-retardant treated wood or open timber assemblies, verify compliance with FPP Appendix B and provide detailed engineering and flame-spread ratings.

🧱 Retaining Walls, Planters & Terraces

🔴 Required [V12 §IV.B.5.i] The maximum permitted height of retaining walls is 2 feet (24 inches) from finished grade. Retaining walls are not permitted adjacent to the existing Residence foundation.
🔴 Required [V12 §IV.B.3] Where soil is mounded or a raised planter/retaining wall occurs, provide required clearances (18" clear of existing perimeter walls) and adequate drainage/waterproofing. Thoroseal or equivalent waterproofing treatment is required where specified.
🔵 Verify Walls exceeding 24 inches or structural site retaining conditions require civil engineering calculations, City grading permits, and major ARC review.

🌳 Tree Placement & Root Barriers

🔴 Required [V12 §IV.D.6] Proposed trees should be kept at least 5'-0" from property lines and boundary fences.
🔴 Required [V12 §IV.D.6] Trees planted within 5'-0" of perimeter walls and/or adjoining residences must incorporate an approved root-barrier device.
🟡 Recommended Show tree species, container size, installed location, and mature canopy diameter on site plans.

🌿 Phase 2 Fire Protection Plan — Prohibited Plants & Trees (Appendix A)

🔴 Required [FPP Appendix A] Zero pyrophytic or high-fuel species permitted. Strictly excludes Italian Cypress, Palms, Pines, Junipers, Bamboo, and Eucalyptus.
🟡 Recommended Select from proven non-pyrophytic trees and ornamentals (Crape Myrtle, Japanese Maple, Western Redbud, Fruit Trees, Star Jasmine).
🔵 Verify Vegetation selection and fuel modification must comply with the complete Phase 2 FPP Appendix A schedule.

💧 Surface Grading & Drainage

🔴 Required [V12 §IV.B.3 / Form G] Maintain established lot drainage patterns. Hardscape surfaces require minimum 1.0% positive drainage; landscape areas require minimum 2.0% positive drainage directed away from structures. Zero runoff or sheet flow onto adjacent lots is permitted.
🔴 Required [V12 §IV.B.3] New drain inlets and sub-drains must connect to the existing drainage system provided by the Declarant.
🟡 Recommended Indicate surface slope arrows, elevations where applicable, and catch basin locations on Sheet L-1.

📋 Product Specifications & Material Cuts (Exhibit G)

🔴 Required [Form B / ARC Review Requirement] Attach visual manufacturer specification sheets, catalog cuts, or material sample swatches for all proposed improvements (pergolas, stone veneer, flatwork, sheds, turf, etc.) to prevent an incomplete package notice.
