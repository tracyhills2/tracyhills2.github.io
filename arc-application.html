HOA Architectural Review Adopted Guidelines (9/16/2025) & Revised Forms

ARC Application & Submittal Guide

A community-built completion aid designed to help Tracy Hills II residents prepare complete, accurate, and guideline-aligned ARC submittals to avoid common review delays.

Watch Video Walkthrough Download Editable ARC Forms & Submittal Package (.docx) View Official Architectural Guidelines (PDF)
Do Not Begin Work Before Written Approval (or Required §II.B Notification): Unless an improvement is specifically Exempt (§II.A) or qualifies as Pre-Approved (§II.B, which does not require prior written ARC approval but still requires filing an application notifying the Association before work begins), the governing documents require written ARC approval before commencing any exterior addition, construction, landscaping, demolition, or modification. Starting work prior to required approval or notification is a violation of Association CC&Rs and may result in fines or mandatory restoration. If work has already started or been completed without required ARC approval, contact Seabreeze Management directly regarding the appropriate compliance process.
ARC Approval vs City of Tracy Permits: ARC approval addresses compliance solely with Association governing documents. It does not replace building permits, zoning compliance, or inspections required by the City of Tracy. Conversely, obtaining a City permit does not waive the requirement for written ARC approval.
Discretionary Review Notice: This guide is a volunteer community reference designed to help residents prepare complete, guideline-aligned packages. It does not guarantee ARC approval. Each lot is reviewed on its specific merits, topography, and conditions. The Association's currently adopted governing documents and official instructions from Seabreeze Management Company always control.

Determine Your Review Classification

Identify which of the four submittal classifications applies to your scope of work before preparing your application package:

Tier 1 $0 Fee

1. Exempt Improvements

Only for improvements specifically identified as exempt under guideline conditions (e.g., minor seasonal planting of annual flowers, original builder touch-up paint, portable patio furniture, potted plants).

Tier 2 Pre-Approved (§II.B)

2. Pre-Approved Improvements

Under §II.B, Pre-Approved improvements do not require prior written ARC approval when they strictly conform to the applicable Guidelines — including rear-yard playground equipment, greenhouses, or storage sheds that do not extend above the fence line, disappearing/invisible screen doors matching trim, and matching replacement garage/entry doors or side-yard gates. However, an application notifying the Association must still be filed before work begins (confirm current notification procedure with Management).

Tier 3 $175 Review Fee

3. Minor Modifications

ARC review for specific minor modifications under §II.C (e.g., repainting home exterior with a different approved color, rear-yard storage sheds that extend above the fence line, or post-mounted pathway lighting).

Tier 4 $200 Fee + $250 Deposit

4. Full / Major Projects

Examples of projects generally requiring Full/Major Review include all new rear/side yard landscaping, concrete patios, retaining walls, pergolas/patio covers, swimming pools, outdoor kitchens, gas fire pits, and water features.

Timelines & Review Deadlines

  • Form A / §III.D
    Initial Landscape Submittal Deadline: Form A states rear yard landscape plans must be submitted within 6 months (180 days) of close of escrow, whereas adopted Guidelines §III.D states 90 days. Submitting within the earlier 90-day window is recommended while Management clarifies this discrepancy.
  • Form A / Form B
    Initial Landscape Completion Deadline: Form A & Form B Part III specify rear yard landscaping must be completed within 9 months (270 days) of close of escrow, whereas adopted Guidelines §III.D states 180 days. Completing within the earlier 180-day window is the conservative approach unless Management confirms otherwise in writing.
  • Form A / Form B
    ARC Review Window: Form B notes the review process typically takes about 30 days, and Form A / §III.D.5 provide the ARC up to 45 calendar days from receipt of a complete package to approve or deny the application. Incomplete submittals will be returned without review.
  • Form B / §III.F
    Construction Window: Per Form B Part III, approved improvements (other than initial rear lot landscaping) must be completed within 6 months of approval.

Submittal Fees & Security Deposit

Review Fee: $200 Full / $175 Minor

Non-refundable review fee payable to Tracy Hills II Community Association ($200 for full landscaping/hardscape, $175 for minor projects).

Security Deposit: $250 (Refundable, subject to §III.D.4 offsets)

Separate check payable to Tracy Hills II Community Association. Returned after the Notice of Completion is approved, subject only to the three permitted offsets in Guidelines §III.D.4: (1) repair of damage to Association Property or an Association Maintenance Area; (2) fines assessed against the Owner's account for a Declaration violation; or (3) legal fees associated with an Owner's Declaration violation.

Application Preparation Steps

1

Complete Official Forms B, C & G (Pages 1–7)

Open the Editable Word (.docx) Package. The document uses three clear visual cues: Plain Black Text is the verbatim HOA form wording, [✏️ Bold Red Text] marks every field, checkbox, and signature line you need to complete, and 💡 Light Blue Dashed Boxes provide helper guidance (delete the blue helper boxes before exporting to PDF).

2

Customize Guided Scope, Turf & Plant Schedules (Attachments 1 & 2)

In Attachment 1 and Attachment 2, replace the bold red [✏️ ☐ Yes] prompts with ☑ Yes for items you are installing, enter your specific dimensions/setbacks, select one of the 4 pre-approved synthetic turf models if applicable, or delete rows you do not need.

3

Insert Plot Plan, Plan Preparer Signature, Site Photos & Cut Sheets (Attachments 3 & 4)

  • • Attachment 3 (Plot Plan & Plan Preparer Acknowledgment): Insert your dimensioned 2D plot/landscape plan (drawn at a scale of not less than 1/8" = 1'-0") showing property lines, residence footprint, setbacks, and drainage slope arrows (1% hardscape / 2% landscape), and complete the Plan Preparer Acknowledgment & Signature block required by §III.D.12.G / §III.E.1.C (if you prepared your own plans, sign as the homeowner/plan preparer).
  • • Attachment 4 Part A (Site Color Photos — Min. 4" × 6"): Per §III.G.5 and §III.H.1, include color photographs (at least 4" × 6" in size) showing the Front Elevation of the residence and lot, Rear Elevation / Rear Yard, each Side Yard, and surrounding conditions adjacent to the proposed improvement (including neighboring lots and Association Property). Add additional photos as needed to clearly show the project location.
  • • Attachment 4 Part B (Materials & Cut Sheets): Paste product photos, color swatches, or manufacturer spec sheets for pavers, pergolas, retaining wall stone, lighting, or sheds.
4

Verify Signatures & Neighbor Notification (Form D Guidance)

The revised Association Forms packet (rev110826) does not include Form D. However, adopted Architectural Guidelines V12, Section III.D.12.C, still references neighbor notification and identifies Adjacent, Facing (3 across the street), and Impacted Neighbors. Residents should confirm with Management whether Form D or another form of neighbor notification is currently required. Unless Management confirms otherwise in writing, consider including the archived Form D courtesy sheet (available in the downloads section above) with your submittal.

Ensure Form B (Part III), Form G, and the Plan Preparer Acknowledgment (Attachment 3) are signed and dated before exporting your package to PDF.

6

Construction & Notice of Completion (Form E)

Build Exactly as Approved: Per Form B Part III, approved improvements (other than initial rear lot landscaping) must be completed within 6 months of approval. For initial rear yard landscaping, Forms A/B state 9 months from close of escrow, whereas adopted Guidelines V12 §III.D state 180 days — completing within the earlier 180-day window is the conservative approach unless Management confirms otherwise in writing. Any deviation from approved plans requires prior written ARC approval.
Notice of Completion (Form E) & Required Post-Construction Photos: Do not include Forms E & F with your initial application. Within 30 days after completing the work, submit Form E along with color photographs documenting all installed improvements — including, as applicable, retaining wall systems, drain inlets, light fixtures, equipment areas, pools, patio covers, backs of fireplaces, and trees/plantings. Per Guidelines §III.A.3, if an improvement involves a setback issue, also include a photograph clearly showing the measurement from the improvement to the property line or applicable setback.
Deposit Refund Process (§III.D.4): Following final inspection/photo review by the ARC confirming substantial conformance, the $250 security deposit is refunded to the homeowner, subject only to the three permitted offsets in §III.D.4: (1) repair of damage to Association Property or an Association Maintenance Area; (2) fines assessed against the Owner's account for a Declaration violation; or (3) legal fees associated with an Owner's Declaration violation.

Essential Standards & Common Delay Factors

Technical specifications organized by requirement level and section citations to help prevent application delays:

Patio Covers, Pergolas & Shade Structures

Required [Guidelines §IV.A.19 & FPP / CBC Ch 7A] Required [§IV.A.19 • FPP • CBC 7A] Requires Full ARC Review. Maximum height: 10 feet for flat-roof structures or 12 feet for sloped/pitched structures; maximum 60% of rear yard coverage (§IV.A.19.G–I). Minimum setbacks: 3'-0" from side property lines; 5'-0" from rear property line (7'-0" from rear line if adjacent to a street).
Recommended Phase 2 properties are subject to the community's Fire Protection Plan (§II.E) and CBC Chapter 7A wildfire ignition-resistance standards. Heavy-gauge aluminum is a widely used non-combustible option (note: §IV.A.19.J states metal patio covers require prior ARC approval, and §IV.A.19.J.V–VII references painted wood beams and min 6"x6" wood or masonry columns). Always include manufacturer spec sheets, color samples, and dimensioned elevations.

Retaining Walls & Planter Curbs

Required [Guidelines §IV.A.18.I] Maximum permitted height of retaining walls and terraced curbs is 24 inches (2 feet) from finished grade. Retaining walls are not permitted adjacent to the existing Residence.
Required [Guidelines §IV.A.18.J] Where soil is mounded against an existing wall or a raised planter occurs, provide required clearance (18" clear of perimeter walls) and adequate waterproofing/drainage. Thoroseal or equivalent waterproofing treatment is required where specified.
Verify Walls exceeding 24 inches or structural site retaining conditions require civil engineering calculations, City grading permits, and major ARC review.

Synthetic Turf & 4 HOA Pre-Approved Turf Brands (§IV.A.28)

Sample Waived [§IV.A.28] Pre-Approved Synthetic Turf Models (No Physical 12"×12" Sample Required): Choosing one of the following four pre-approved brands/models waives the requirement to submit a physical turf sample (an ARC application and plot plan are still required):
• Imperial Synthetic Turf (IST): California Royal 89
• Boone Action Turf: Irvine Fescue One, Base Hit 55, Base Hit 85
• Pro Green Synthetic Grass: Natural Real 80, Natural Real 60, Natural Real 50
• Tiger Turf: Diamond Pro Fescue, Diamond Pro Spring, Diamond Supreme Fescue, Diamond Supreme Spring
Required [§IV.A.28] If proposing any other synthetic turf brand, you must submit a 12"×12" physical sample and specifications meeting §IV.A.28 (min 60 oz face weight, 1.75"–2.5" pile height, min 8-year warranty, non-toxic infill, and min 18" buffer from neighbor's natural turf).

Tree Setbacks & Root Barriers

Required [§IV.A.17.1.I] Tree Setback: Proposed trees should be kept at least 5'-0" from property lines and boundary fences.
Required [§IV.A.27.6] Root Barrier: Trees planted within 5 feet of perimeter walls and/or adjoining residences must incorporate an approved root-barrier device.
Recommended Show tree species, container size, installed location, and mature canopy diameter on site plans.

Phase 2 Fire Protection Plan: Prohibited Plants & Trees (Appendix A)

Required [§II.E / FPP Appendix A] Proposed vegetation must comply with the complete Fire Protection Plan prohibited-plant schedule. Prominent examples include Italian Cypress, Palms, Pines, Junipers, Bamboo, and Eucalyptus. Verify every proposed species against the official schedule below.
Recommended Select from proven non-pyrophytic trees and ornamentals (Crape Myrtle, Japanese Maple, Western Redbud, Fruit Trees, Star Jasmine).

Drainage & Grading

Required [§IV.A.17.2 & .3 / Form G] Maintain established lot drainage patterns. Hardscape surfaces require minimum 1.0% positive drainage; landscape areas require minimum 2.0% positive drainage directed away from structures. Do not direct runoff or sheet flow onto adjacent lots. New drainage inlets and sub-drains must connect to the existing drainage system provided by the Declarant, as required by the Architectural Guidelines.
Recommended Indicate surface slope arrows, elevations where applicable, and catch basin locations on your Plot Plan.

Site Color Photos & Finish / Materials Schedule (Form A & Form B)

Required [Form A / Form B] Site Color Photos: Include color photographs of the Front Yard showing the entire house and yard (explicitly required on Form A), Rear Yard, and both Side Yards.
Required [Form B Part II] Finish & Materials Schedule: Provide manufacturer specification sheets, catalog cuts, and material/color photos for proposed items (pergolas, pavers, stone veneer, lighting, sheds, etc.) in Attachment 4 to prevent an incomplete submittal return.
