Key Homeowner Deadlines

  • • Rear Yard Submittal Deadline: Initial landscape plans must be submitted within 6 months of close of escrow.
  • • Rear Yard Completion Deadline: All rear yard landscaping must be completed within 9 months (180 days) of close of escrow.
  • • ARC Review Window: The ARC has up to 45 calendar days (typically ~30 days) to review and approve/deny completed packages.

Step-by-Step Instructions: Preparing Your Application

1

Update Bracketed Red Text

Open the Word document and replace all red placeholders (e.g., [LIKE THIS]) with your specific details: full name, street address, lot number, tract number, phone, email, and neighbor names/addresses.

2

Select Your Improvements (Modular Menu)

Review Section 2 and Form C. Mark [X] Yes for items you are installing and [X] No for items you are not installing. The pre-written text complies with City and HOA guidelines (extruded aluminum pergolas, 24-inch retaining wall limits, 5-foot tree setbacks, and positive drainage).

3

Drop in Drawings, Lot Photos & Product Cuts

  • • Exhibit E (Drawings): Insert your dimensioned 2D site layout (Sheet L-1) showing lot lines, setbacks, and drainage arrows. Include Name, Address, Lot #, and North Arrow.
  • • Exhibit F (Existing Site Photos): Add 4 clear photos of current lot (front elevation, entire backyard, left side yard, right side yard).
  • • Exhibit G (Product Cuts & Swatches): Paste catalog cuts, spec sheets, or material swatches into the boxes for selected amenities, and delete unused boxes.
4

Collect Neighbor Signatures & Execute Forms

Review plan drawings with adjacent, rear, and facing neighbors to obtain signatures on Exhibit C (Form D) (signatures verify notification, not approval; write "Builder/Vacant" if lot is unoccupied). Sign and date Form B (Checklist), Form C (Request), and Form G (Water Quality BMP Agreement).

5

Submit to Seabreeze Management

Convert your finalized Word document to a clean PDF and email it to csteam@seabreezemgmt.com.

📬 Mailing Address for Checks: Tracy Hills II Community Association c/o Seabreeze Management Company, 4305 Hacienda Suite 350, Pleasanton CA 94588.

Top 5 Rejection Triggers to Avoid

Rule 1 Combustible Patio Covers

Wood patio covers are strictly prohibited under the Phase 2 Fire Protection Plan. Pergolas and patio covers must be constructed of heavy-gauge extruded aluminum (powder-coated).

Rule 2 Wall Heights Over 24 Inches

Retaining walls and terraced planter curbs must never exceed 24 inches from finished grade. Walls must be Thoroseal waterproofed and contain an internal sub-drain.

Rule 3 Tree Setbacks Under 5 Feet

All proposed trees must maintain a minimum 5′-0″ setback clearance from property lines, fences, and structures, and utilize root barrier collars.

Rule 4 Prohibited Flammable Planting

Never include pyrophytic plant species prohibited under Appendix 'A' of the Fire Protection Plan (zero cypress, palms, pines, junipers, bamboo, or eucalyptus).

Rule 5 Runoff & Sheet Flow Rules

Slabs must slope 1.0% away from house; landscape beds must slope 2.0% to area drains. All sub-drains must tie directly into builder PVC storm lines—never sheet-flowing into neighboring yards.
