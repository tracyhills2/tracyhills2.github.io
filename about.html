Community Information Hub

About This Website

An independent, resident-run reference portal bringing together helpful community contacts, builder warranty guidance, city service links, and local neighborhood recommendations for Tracy Hills Phase II.

Why This Website Exists

Moving into a new construction neighborhood like Tracy Hills II involves navigating multiple systems: Seabreeze HOA submittals, Lennar builder warranty procedures, City of Tracy municipal services, PG&E solar interconnection, and finding reliable local contractors.

This website brings together helpful community information and resources in one convenient, searchable place to make them easier for Phase II residents to find and explore.

Independent Community Resource

This is an independent community resource created by resident volunteers. It is NOT an official website of:

  • Tracy Hills II Community Association (HOA)
  • Seabreeze Management Company
  • Lennar Homes or associated developers
  • City of Tracy or San Joaquin County
  • Any government agency or commercial service provider

For official binding requests, payments, and architectural submittals, residents should always access the official portals linked throughout the website.

Content Maintenance & Verification

Information on this website is periodically reviewed against official sources. Information items feature Last Verified or Last Updated dates (most recently verified: August 22, 2026).

Community members are encouraged to help keep the portal accurate. If you notice outdated phone numbers, changed HOA procedures, or broken links, please visit our Report Incorrect Information page.
