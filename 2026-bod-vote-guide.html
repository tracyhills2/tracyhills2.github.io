Tracy Hills II - 2026 Board Election Voting Guide

Step-by-step homeowner resource for completing and returning your mail-in ballot

Last reviewed: September 16, 2026
⚠️

Independent resident guide - not the election administrator. Your official election packet controls. If this guide differs from your packet, follow the official packet and contact Pro Elections LLC with questions.

This guide explains how to complete, package, and return your ballot. It does not endorse candidates, provide legal advice, or process ballots.
Step 1 of 5

Check Your Packet

Verify that your election packet contains these four items before marking your ballot:

1. Delivery Envelope

Outer envelope labeled "IMPORTANT: BALLOT ENCLOSED".

2. Ballot & Instructions

Two-sided paper sheet with candidates on front and rules/meeting info on back.

3. Secret Ballot Envelope

Inner teal envelope labeled "SECRET BALLOT ENVELOPE".

4. Return Envelope

White Business Reply envelope with pre-paid postage and voter signature lines.

Step 2 of 5

Mark Your Ballot

There are two (2) open seats on the Board of Directors. Follow these direct marking instructions:

  • ✓ Select up to 2 candidates.
  • ✓ Fill each selected circle completely, matching the solid-dot example on the ballot (●).
  • ✓ Use a pen. Do not use pencil.
  • ✕ Do not use an X or checkmark.
Step 3 of 5

Seal the Secret Ballot Envelope

  • ● Place your completed ballot inside the teal Secret Ballot Envelope and seal it shut.
  • ● Do not write or sign anything on the Secret Ballot Envelope. Keep it free of identifying information.
Step 4 of 5

Complete & Seal the Return Envelope

  • 1. Place the sealed teal Secret Ballot Envelope inside the Return Envelope.
  • 2. Complete and sign the required information on the designated lines on the back flap (Owner Name, Tracy Hills II Property Address, and Signature), as instructed in your official election packet.
  • 3. Seal the Return Envelope shut.
Step 5 of 5

Mail Your Ballot via USPS

  • ✉️ Place the sealed Return Envelope in the USPS mail.
  • 📬 No stamp or postage is required - the envelope has prepaid Business Reply postage.
Strict Receipt Deadline
Mail early. Your ballot must be RECEIVED by Pro Elections LLC by October 15, 2026 at 2:00 PM.
Suggested mail-by date: October 7, 2026 to allow sufficient USPS transit time. October 7 is a suggested date; October 15 at 2:00 PM is the actual receipt deadline.

1. Delivery Envelope

Key Packet Information:

  • This is the outer mailer envelope sent to your registered address by Pro Elections LLC.
  • Clearly printed with "IMPORTANT: BALLOT ENCLOSED" in bold lettering.

2. Ballot & Instructions Sheet

The Front Side contains the candidate selection list for the 2 open Board seats. Fill in up to two (2) bubbles completely following the marking example on the official ballot.

The Back Side contains the official election instructions, quorum information, voter requirements, and meeting information.

3. Secret Ballot Envelope (Teal)

Envelope Requirements:

  • Labeled "SECRET BALLOT ENVELOPE" in distinct teal/blue paper.
  • Fold your completed paper ballot and place it inside, then seal it completely shut.
  • IMPORTANT: Do not write or sign anything on the Secret Ballot Envelope. Keep the ballot envelope free of identifying information.

4. Return Envelope (White)

Return Envelope Requirements:

  • White envelope with pre-paid Business Reply postage addressed to Pro Elections LLC in Murphys, CA.
  • Insert your sealed teal Secret Ballot Envelope inside this Return Envelope.
  • REQUIRED FIELDS ON BACK: Complete and sign the required information on the designated lines on the back flap (Owner Name, Tracy Hills II Property Address, and Signature), as instructed in your official election packet.
