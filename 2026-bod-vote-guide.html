Step 1 of 4

Inspect Your Election Packet

Your official election envelope arrived via US Mail. Verify that your packet contains all four required items before marking your vote:

1. Delivery Envelope

Outer envelope labeled "IMPORTANT: BALLOT ENCLOSED". Can be recycled once inner items are verified.

2. Ballot & Instructions

Two-sided official paper sheet. Front lists candidate options; back details rules, quorum & meeting info.

3. Secret Ballot Envelope

Inner teal/blue envelope labeled "SECRET BALLOT ENVELOPE". Protects voter anonymity.

4. Outer Return Envelope

White envelope labeled "RETURN ENVELOPE" with pre-paid Business Reply postage and homeowner signature lines.

Step 2 of 4

Mark Your Ballot Correctly

Follow the marking instructions included with your official ballot. There are two (2) open seats on the Board of Directors. You may select up to two candidates. Follow the official ballot instructions for how to submit a ballot if you do not wish to select a candidate.

OFFICIAL BALLOT MARKING INSTRUCTIONS
  • ● Use blue or black ink pen only as specified on the ballot.
  • ● Completely fill in the circle next to your chosen candidates solid (●).
  • ● Select up to two (2) candidates total.
MARKING ERRORS TO AVOID
  • ✕ DO NOT use pencil, red ink, or highlighters.
  • ✕ DO NOT use checkmarks (✓) or "X" marks; bubbles must be filled solid.
  • ✕ DO NOT select more than 2 choices. Overvoting invalidates candidate selections per official instructions.
Step 3 of 4

The 2-Envelope Packaging System

Follow the two-envelope process shown in your official Tracy Hills II election packet. The packet uses a two-envelope process to keep the ballot separate from the homeowner identification information used for verification.

Envelope #1 (Inner) TEAL COLOR

"SECRET BALLOT ENVELOPE"

Fold your marked paper ballot and place it inside this teal envelope, then seal it shut securely.

DO NOT WRITE OR SIGN anything on the teal envelope!
Envelope #2 (Outer) WHITE COLOR

"RETURN ENVELOPE"

Insert the sealed teal Secret Ballot Envelope inside this white Business Reply envelope.

REQUIRED: Fill out Owner Name, Tracy Hills II Address, and SIGN the back flap!

Common Election Questions & Troubleshooting

Didn't receive your ballot?

If you have not received your official election packet, or if your packet is damaged or missing an item, contact the Official Inspector of Elections, Pro Elections LLC, using the official contact information provided in your election materials.

Made a mistake on your ballot?

Don't guess or make additional changes to the ballot. Contact Pro Elections LLC using the official election contact information and ask how to obtain a replacement ballot or how to proceed.

1. Outer Delivery Envelope

Key Packet Information:

  • This is the outer mailer envelope sent to your registered address by Pro Elections LLC.
  • Clearly printed with "IMPORTANT: BALLOT ENCLOSED" in bold lettering.
  • Once you open this package and verify that all inner materials (Ballot sheet, Teal Secret Ballot Envelope, and White Return Envelope) are present, this outer mailer can be recycled.

2. Ballot & Instructions Sheet

The Front Side contains the candidate selection list for the 2 open Board seats. Fill in up to two (2) bubbles completely using a blue or black pen.

The Back Side outlines statutory California voting rules, quorum requirements (367 ballots), voter qualification criteria, and meeting logistics.

3. Inner Secret Ballot Envelope (Teal)

Envelope Requirements:

  • Labeled "SECRET BALLOT ENVELOPE" in distinct teal/blue paper.
  • Fold your completed paper ballot and place it inside, then seal it completely shut.
  • CRITICAL RULE: Do NOT sign or write your name, address, or any identifying marks on this teal envelope. Any writing on this envelope voids secrecy!

4. Outer Return Envelope (White)

Outer Return Envelope Requirements:

  • Outer white envelope with pre-paid Business Reply postage addressed to Pro Elections LLC in Murphys, CA.
  • Insert your sealed teal Secret Ballot Envelope inside this outer envelope.
  • REQUIRED FIELDS ON BACK: You must print your Owner Name, your Tracy Hills II Property Address, and affix your official SIGNATURE on the designated lines on the back flap.
  • Unsigned return envelopes cannot be verified by the Election Inspector and will be voided!
