Report Incorrect Information

Help keep the Tracy Hills II Community Resource Portal accurate, reliable, and up to date for all Phase II neighborhood residents.

How to Submit a Correction

If you find a phone number that has changed, an outdated HOA fee schedule, a broken portal link, or an updated contractor listing, please let us know.

Please include the following details in your report:

  • Page or Section Name: (e.g. Quick Contacts, Community FAQ - ARC Approval, Service Directory - Plumbers)
  • Description of the Issue: (e.g. Outdated phone number, changed office hours, incorrect website link)
  • Suggested Correction: (e.g. The updated telephone number is 800-XXX-XXXX)
  • Official Source or Reference: (e.g. Seabreeze HOA email broadcast, City of Tracy official site link)

Correction Assistant Form

Fill out the fields below and click "Prepare Email Correction" to generate a formatted email to tracyhillsphase2@gmail.com.

Correction Review Process: Submission of a correction request does not guarantee automatic modification of website content. All suggested updates are reviewed against official organization sources or verified with community contacts prior to publishing.
